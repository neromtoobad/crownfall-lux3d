## Fields

**Project name**

Crownfall, rebuilt with Aholo Lux3D

**One line**

A playable 3D medieval realm in the browser where every castle, wall, tower, house and hall was generated from text with the Lux3D API.

**Description**

Crownfall is a 3D medieval realm you can walk, race chariots and scheme in. Its layout is code: it decides where every wall segment, tower and house goes and stretches one model into each slot, so the whole built world comes from six 3D models. For this hackathon all six were generated from text with Aholo Lux3D (G1-Turbo) through the Aholo API, then fitted and dropped into the game by one script. The market barrels, crates, wells and hay bales are Lux3D generations too, which makes 10 models in all.

Crownfall is our own game, started before this hackathon. For this event we rebuilt its world with Lux3D: the 10 models, the generation and fitting script, the "how it was built" page and this report.
