## Fields

**Project name**

Crownfall, rebuilt with Aholo Lux3D

**One line**

A playable 3D medieval realm in the browser where every castle, wall, tower, house and hall was generated from text with the Lux3D API.

**Description**

Crownfall is a 3D medieval realm you can walk, race chariots and scheme in. Its layout is code: it decides where every wall segment, tower and house goes and stretches one model into each slot, so the whole built world comes from six 3D models. For this hackathon all six were generated from text with Aholo Lux3D (G1-Turbo) through the Aholo API, then fitted and dropped into the game by one script. A switch in the header flips the same realm between the Lux3D models and the models they replaced, so you can compare the two worlds directly. Total generation time for the whole realm's architecture: 13 minutes.

Crownfall the game existed before this event (built 8 to 15 November 2026 for a different hackathon). What was made for this event is the Lux3D rebuild of its world, the generation and fitting script, the model switch, the "how it was built" page and the report.
