Crownfall, rebuilt with Aholo Lux3D

Crownfall is a 3D medieval realm you can walk, race and scheme in. Every castle, wall, tower, house and hall in it is placed from six 3D models. For the Manycore Aholo Global 3D AI Hackathon, each of those models was generated from a text prompt with the Lux3D API.

How it works

  1. One prompt per kit model. The prompts live in tools/lux3d/kit.json and describe the realm's look: pale limestone, terracotta roofs, timber frames.
  2. Lux3D text-to-3D. tools/lux3d/kit.mjs creates a task with POST /lux3d/v1/generate/text-to-3d/task/create, polls GET /lux3d/v1/generate/task/get, and downloads the GLB. Lux3D runs one generation at a time, so the script is a serial queue.
  3. Fit for the realm. The same script joins the model into one mesh, keeps its base colour texture, simplifies it to the realm's triangle budget and compresses it, because each model is drawn hundreds of times.
  4. Placed by the game. web/src/realm/Props.tsx stretches each model into every slot the realm's layout asks for, as one instanced draw call per model.

What Lux3D made, and what we made for this event

  • Lux3D: the keep of every house, every wall tower and gatehouse, every stretch of wall, every cottage and townhouse, and the hall of the Ashen Throne. The market barrels, crates, wells and hay bales are Lux3D too.
  • The project: Crownfall is our own game, started before this hackathon. For this event we rebuilt its world with Lux3D: the 10 models, the generation and fitting script, this page and the report.
